## 2. What worked

- **Joining was fast.** Big code on the TV, one box on the phone, typed four letters, seats appeared. Aisyah (11) got in with her mother's help, no fuss. I never needed the QR.
- **The seat cards** ("WHO YOU ARE / WHAT YOU WANT") were short enough that even I read them. Everyone knew their job in one sentence.
- **"TWO LINES YOU CAN SAY"** was genuinely fun — every family member read theirs out loud and started using them on each other within a minute ("Explain it to me like I have to pay for it. Because I do.").
- **The arrows on cards** (▼▼▼ / ▲▲▲, green vs red, "Dirty card" in bold) made the choice instant. I did not have to read the description to know Make It Here was the best card.
- **"TAP A CARD FIRST" → "LOCK MY CARD" → "Locked. Now look up."** Very clear two-step; nobody in the family locked by accident.
- **The send-money flow** explained itself: "They must accept it. Once they do, the money is theirs. They do not have to pay it back." Suraya's phone said "INCOMING OFFER … You owe nothing back" and she pressed ACCEPT. Good.
- **The practice round** let the kids burn a veto and a Spotlight "for fun" and get them back, and they noticed they came back. That is the right way to teach.
- **Space / n / p keys** on the laptop worked exactly as the TV said, and the TV always said which key did what.
- **The reveal's "BROKE THE PROMISE ✕ / BOTH KEPT THE DEAL ✓"** created a real family moment — kids shouting, wife tutting. That is the game working.

## 3. What did not work

1. **You promise blind.** During "THE TALK" the phone lets you say "The Government will pick "Defend the Ringga"." but shows only the three card names — no cost, no arrows, no "Dirty card" label. Suraya said out loud: *"We all only see card names, not what they do, so we are all promising blind."* Then when THE CHOICE started I saw Defend the Ringga was ECONOMY ▼▼ and switched. Result: **"BROKE THE PROMISE ✕"** on the TV for me and Aisyah, both of whom promised before seeing the cards. Aisyah: *"Not fair, I only promised because I couldn't see the arrows!"* This is the biggest problem I hit.
2. **The practice reveal disappeared before I looked up.** The phone said "Locked. Now look up." I did, a few seconds later, and the TV was already on "YOUR POWER 0:27". Danial said the same: *"I missed the reveal, the TV already changed to 'YOUR POWER' by the time I looked up. So fast."* Three out of four of us missed it. Only Suraya saw "100 to 53".
3. **The pause/next control is only on the laptop.** I was the only one who could press n / Space / p, so I was the bottleneck and I also happened to be the most impatient one. I started the session with Suraya not ready ("4 OF 4 · 3 READY") because the TV said "SPACE STARTS THE SESSION" and did not stop me.
4. **Public Trust made no sense to the family.** TV said "Nobody hands these out. The people give them to whoever helped them most." Then both points went to Government, who had just been marked "BROKE THE PROMISE". Meanwhile Aisyah's phone told her *"Cut carbon by 11.1 million tonnes. The biggest carbon cut this round"* and *"The Government or Business helped, so it worked twice as well"* — yet the TV showed "PUBLIC TRUST HELD Government 2 · Business 0 · Activist 0" with **Community not even listed**. Danial: *"This game is rigged for the Minister lah."* Also Danial's 1 point from sharing his tip appeared to vanish ("Activist 0"). Nobody could explain it.
5. **Carbon numbers did not add up for us.** TV said carbon went 100 → 58 (a 42 drop). My phone said "Cut carbon by 9.5 million tonnes", Suraya's "9.3", Aisyah's "11.1". That is about 30, not 42. And in practice the TV said 100 → 53 then everyone's phone reset to 100 without explanation; Aisyah: *"the 53 was fake."*
6. **SEND 1 / SEND 2 looked greyed out** when the sheet opened even though Business was the only possible recipient. I had to tap "Business" first to wake them up. Small, but I stared at it.
7. **Practice "does not count" but visibly spent things.** Aisyah: *"my VETOES says 1 now. It said practice does not count! Did I lose one for real??"* Danial: *"it says 2 LEFT at the bottom but top still says 3."* They did come back, but for a full minute two kids thought they had wasted their powers.
8. **The Spotlight never asks who.** Danial, twice: *"it never asked me WHO to spotlight, it just says 'Spotlight is on'."* He did not understand what he had done.
9. **Waiting.** Talk phase is 2:00. We were all done in about 50 seconds and then sat there. Danial: *"so boring waiting… Dad can you skip?"* I could (n), but nobody else could.
10. **HOW TO PLAY on the phone** did nothing for Suraya on the first screen (*"I pressed HOW TO PLAY on my phone and nothing happened"*). Later the ⋯ opened a long page that Aisyah closed immediately: *"so many words in here, I cannot read all this."*
11. **Suraya's deal wording.** She wanted to say "I will pick X if you pay half" but the phone only offered "if the Government also picks a good card". So the actual deal we made out loud was not the one on the TV.

## 4. Words or phrases I did not understand or had to guess

- **"Public Trust"** — never explained on my phone before it mattered. I only learned from the TV after the fact, and the rule ("Some cards need them") is still unclear to me.
- **"It breaks moving together"** / "moving together bonus" — I guessed it means everyone-picks-clean bonus; Suraya found the definition in the ⋯ page, I never did.
- **"Clean Economy 10%"** — a fourth number that is not one of the three targets. Why is it there?
- **"Partnership"** and **"pay half"** — I did it because my wife told me to, not because I understood what "Without it the partnership only half works" would do to the numbers.
- **"needs 5.0 a round from here"** — I read this as "needs 5.0 more per round" which scared me; I think it means the average must be 5.0.
- **"Every 1% of growth now adds 0.8 million tonnes of carbon."** — I skimmed past this. No idea what to do with it.
- **"Steady Hand — The economy never grows less than 4% in any round."** — took it as "keep economy above 4"; fine, but "grows less than" is awkward English.
- **"Ringga"** — Aisyah asked; we guessed Ringgit.
- **"FACILITATOR SCRIPT F"** — no idea what a facilitator is in a family living room. Did not press it.
- **"IF NOTHING CHANGES, IN 2050 122 Mt … 122 million tonnes to go"** — is 122 the carbon in 2050 or the amount to cut? Both readings fit.

## 5. Top 5 changes I would ask for

1. **Show the arrows and cost of my three cards during THE TALK** (or at least on the "I will pick…" list). Nobody should be punished with "BROKE THE PROMISE" for a promise made before they could see the card.
2. **Hold the reveal until the facilitator presses Next**, or keep the four cards on screen for a fixed 20–30 seconds with a visible countdown, and make the phone buzz/say "LOOK UP NOW". The reveal is the best part and three of us missed it in practice.
3. **Put a one-line "why" under each Public Trust award and list all four seats** ("Community 0"). If the Community can never hold Public Trust, say so on Aisyah's phone at the start. Right now the biggest carbon cutter got nothing and the promise-breaker got everything, and the family thought the game was broken.
4. **Let the round advance when all four phones tap "Done talking" / "Ready"**, not only from the laptop. I was the remote control and the bottleneck; the kids were bored waiting on a 2:00 timer.
5. **Make practice visibly free**: show "PRACTICE — nothing is spent" on the counters, keep the top-corner number unchanged, and reset the country numbers on screen with a line like "Practice over. Back to 100." Also make Spotlight ask *who* (or say plainly "hits whoever picks a dirty card, no need to choose").

Bonus: a plain "GO / JOIN" button after typing the code; grey buttons should not appear grey when they are actually tappable (SEND 1/2).

Would I play again? Yes, next weekend, if it keeps to 35 minutes and the promise-blind problem is fixed — that one made two of the four players feel cheated in the very first real round, and an 11-year-old does not forget "not fair". I would also want the pause/next off my laptop so I can play instead of being the projector man.
