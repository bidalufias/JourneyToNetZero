## 2. What worked

- Joining was fast. Type four letters, seat list appears, tap seat, type name. No account, no password. Faster than most mobile games.
- The seat boxes with `WHO YOU ARE` / `WHAT YOU WANT` in two lines. I read those and I actually knew what my job was.
- `TWO LINES YOU CAN SAY` — everyone in the family read theirs out loud and laughed. That was the best 30 seconds of the setup.
- `TAP A CARD FIRST` → `LOCK MY CARD` → `Locked. Now look up.` I never wondered what to press on the choice screen.
- The `SAY IT` menu with three sentence types (`I will pick…`, `I will pick… if you…`, `I want you to…`). Building a deal was three taps and it showed up on the TV with my name on it. Felt like a real move.
- The tip screen. `THIS IS TRUE` in a yellow bar, red `SHARE IT` button, and it told me exactly what I get (`You get 1 Public Trust`). I decided in one second.
- `BROKE THE PROMISE ✕` in red on the big screen. Best moment of the night, because it was Dad and Aisyah, not me.
- The cards in practice and the real round are the same style, so the practice actually taught me the lock button.

## 3. What did not work

- **Spotlight in practice looked like it was used up.** I pressed `USE SPOTLIGHT` and the button said `SPOTLIGHT · 2 LEFT` while the header said `SPOTLIGHTS 3`. Two numbers, two answers. Aisyah had the same panic with her vetoes (`my VETOES says 1 now`). Mum had to say "we check after practice". The screen that said `This does not count` should have kept the number at 3.
- **Spotlight never asks WHO.** The ⋯ guide said `You name one player.` But the popup was just `USE SPOTLIGHT / CANCEL` and then `Spotlight is on.` I said out loud twice "it never asks me who". I still do not know if it hits everyone or nobody.
- **"Protest card" is not marked on any card.** The spotlight says `It only works if you also pick a protest card this round.` My three cards said `Free` and nothing else. I guessed `Name the Funds Betting Against Us` was a protest. No idea if I was right, because nobody went dirty so nothing happened.
- **Missed the practice reveal completely.** Phone said `Look up.` I looked up and the TV was already on `YOUR POWER`. Dad said the same: "the TV went so fast, I didn't even see the cards." The one screen where all four cards are shown is the one that disappeared fastest.
- **Deals are made blind.** In `THE TALK` the `I will pick…` menu only shows card NAMES, no arrows. I promised `Name the Funds Betting Against Us`, then in `YOUR CHOICE` I saw it has `ECONOMY ▼▼` and `Join the Economic Council` was all green. I kept my promise and got the worse card. Dad and Aisyah broke theirs and got the better cards AND Dad got both Public Trust points. Aisyah's exact words: "Not fair, I only promised because I couldn't see the arrows!"
- **Public Trust made no sense.** I got `You get 1 Public Trust` for sharing the tip. End of round: `Activist 0`. Dad broke his promise and got `Government 2`. Aisyah had `The biggest carbon cut this round` and got nothing. Nobody in the room could explain it. Mum said "that seems unfair".
- **The talk phase is long and empty.** After three taps I had nothing to do for about 60 seconds. `SEND MONEY` is grey for me and stays grey with no reason on screen. Aisyah said "so boring, one minute more" and I agreed.
- **Nothing on the choice screen says which card is a "join them" card or "dirty card".** `Join the Economic Council` had `You work with them. You gain power. Some supporters leave you.` That sounds like the "join them" thing from the `No Compromise` goal but it never says so.
- **The ⋯ page is a wall.** Aisyah: "so many words in here, I cannot read all this." I read three lines and closed it. The two things I needed (who does the spotlight hit, what is a protest card) I could not find quickly.
- **`Read the news. Nothing to tap yet.`** — a whole screen with a timer and nothing to do, twice per round.

## 5. Top 5 changes I would ask for

1. **Show the arrows in the talk phase.** Let me see the cards before I promise one. Right now honest players get punished and liars get points.
2. **Don't take away spotlights/vetoes in practice**, or at least keep both numbers the same. The header said 3, the button said 2, the whole family got confused for a minute.
3. **Make the spotlight ask "Who?"** with four faces to tap, and put a little `PROTEST` tag on the cards that count as protest cards. Right now I'm pressing a button that says `Spotlight is on` and hoping.
4. **Hold the reveal screen longer** or put a `NEXT` on it that Dad has to press. It's the best screen in the game and it vanished before I looked up. Also show it on my phone, not just `Look up.`
5. **Explain Public Trust in one line when it's given.** "Government got 2 because…" Otherwise it looks rigged for the Minister, and the promise-breaker getting rewarded makes the `BROKE THE PROMISE ✕` feel pointless.

Bonus: give me something to tap during the talk when I've already said my thing, and let Dad skip the `Read the news` countdown.

## 6. Was it fun?

Parts, yes. Making a deal that appears on the TV with my name, seeing `BROKE THE PROMISE ✕` on Dad and Aisyah, the secret goal, the secret tip — those were good. Beating Aisyah on the tick/cross was worth it even if she keeps shouting "11.1!"

Boring bits: the briefing (30 seconds of reading on the TV), the talk phase after you've said your one sentence, and `Read the news. Nothing to tap yet.` The ⋯ page is homework.

Would I play again? Yeah, if Dad puts it on. But I'd want the spotlight to actually catch someone, because right now my whole power did nothing in round 1 and I still don't know how it works. Also it's a bit unfair that the Activist has no money and the Minister gets everything, but that's probably the point.
