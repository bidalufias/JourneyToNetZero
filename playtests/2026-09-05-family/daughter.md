## 2. What worked (I knew exactly what to do)

- Typing the code. I typed KXXC and it just went, I didn't need to find a "go" button.
- The seat boxes. Colours and two lines each, easy. Mum said "tap the orange one" and it was orange.
- `I AM READY` big button at the bottom. Easy.
- `Look up.` in big letters. I knew to look at the TV.
- The cards with arrows. Green ▲ good, red ▼ bad. I could pick the one with the most green even without reading everything. The words `Dirty card` scared me off, which I think is the point.
- `TAP A CARD FIRST` turned into `LOCK MY CARD` after I tapped a card, so I knew what to do next.
- The ✓ on the card after I tapped it.
- SAY IT menus. Tap, tap, tap, then pick a person. It appeared on the TV straight away and Danial saw it. That was fun.
- The secret goal. I liked having a secret from Danial.
- My phone result page told me MY card was the biggest cut. I liked that.

## 3. What did not work

- **Practice took my veto.** Phone said `This does not count.` but when I said no to Danial, `VETOES` at the top went from `2` to `1` and the popup said `You will have 1 veto left.` I thought I lost it for real. It came back later but nobody told me it would. Danial had the same with his Spotlight (`2 LEFT at the bottom but top still says 3`).
- **Slider.** `SLIDE ALL THE WAY TO SAY NO`. Everything else is tap, tap, tap, and then suddenly one thing is a slide. I nearly ran out of time on it.
- **Promising blind.** In THE TALK, `I will pick…` only showed the card NAMES: `Switch to Local Food`, `Demand Price Controls`, `Neighbourhood Buying Groups`. No arrows. I promised Local Food because it sounded nice. Then in YOUR CHOICE it had red arrows! So I changed, and the TV put `BROKE THE PROMISE ✕` on my card in front of everyone and Danial laughed. Dad had the same thing. That felt like a trick. How can I promise a card if I can't see what it does?
- **Card names I don't understand.** `Demand Price Controls`, `Neighbourhood Buying Groups`. What is a buying group? I picked it for the arrows, not because I knew what it was.
- **Waiting.** THE TALK is two minutes. After everybody said one thing, the phone just says `Nobody has said anything yet` or the same list, and there is nothing to do. Danial and I both said "boring". Then the TV said `THE CRISIS` for 21 seconds and my phone said `Read the news. Nothing to tap yet.`
- **The three dots page is too long.** TV said `Tap ⋯ to see what a word means.` I tapped it and got a giant page with `WHAT YOU SPEND`, `HOW A ROUND GOES`, `WORDS ON YOUR SCREEN`, `THE FULL WRITTEN GUIDE`, `LEAVE THE GAME`. - **Carbon 53 in practice then back to 100.** I thought we did really well and then it was all fake. Confusing.

## 6. Was it fun?

Some of it was fun. The best bits were seeing my sentence appear on the TV so Danial had to read it, picking the card with the most green arrows, my card being on the big TV in orange, having a secret goal, and my phone saying I did the biggest carbon cut (11.1!). Would I play again? Yes, if Danial plays, because I want to get the tick and beat him next time. But I want Mum next to me to read the words. And I want to play the whole game, not go to bed after round 2.
