## 2. What worked (I knew exactly what to do)

- Typing the code. The box said "CODE" in big letters, easy.
- The seat cards. "WHO YOU ARE" and "WHAT YOU WANT" are short and I could read them.
- The big red "SAY IT" button and the big green "I AM DONE" button. I always knew which one to press.
- The phone TELLS you the next thing: "Good. Everyone can see it on the big screen. Now tap I AM DONE." and "Locked. Now look up." I like that a lot.
- Green arrows = good, red arrows = bad. I could pick a card just by counting green arrows.
- "Look up." when the TV is talking. I knew to look at the TV.
- My sentence appeared on the TV. That was cool. And getting a "✓" for "BOTH KEPT THE DEAL" felt like winning.
- The clock at the top so I knew to hurry.

- "HOW TO PLAY" – Mum said she pressed it and "nothing opened". So nobody in my family read how to play.
- The Activist card has "Your card is always under ⋯, top right." I never found out what ⋯ is. Abang said "the three dots got one whole essay inside. Too long lah."
- The first screen after "I AM READY" just showed names and "YOU" and no button. I waited for Mum for a long time. Boring.
- **The TV reveal in practice**: it showed Ayah's and Abang's cards but MY box and Mum's were black and empty. I shouted "Where is my card?" Then it flipped later. It should say "flipping…" or something.
- "Practice · Step 3 of 4" showed me the SAME Activist card I read at the start. Abang said "Same card as before, I already read it lah." Boring again.
- The secret goal: I read "Justice First... one of your Spotlights catches someone" and I said it out loud by accident because it was exciting. The phone says "Nobody sees which one you took" but nothing tells a kid "don't say it out loud".
- I had to choose between the card with the most green arrows ("Rewrite the Loan Conditions") and the card my Spotlight works with ("Reject the Conditions"). The phone said "Spotlight is on. Pick a protest card, or it does nothing." I did not know which was right and Ayah said one thing and Mum said another. There was a clock. Stressful.

- Mum said her cards only appeared AFTER she pressed "I AM DONE TALKING", but mine showed inside SAY IT. So we all saw different things and got confused talking to each other.
- Ayah and Abang argued the whole time about money ("my budget still says 4", "why minus 2?"). I don't have money so I just sat there.

## 5. My top 5 things I would change

1. **Put a little picture on every card and every crisis.** A bus, a factory with smoke, a bank, a lightning bolt for blackout. I pick by picture. Words like "Rewrite the Loan Conditions" mean nothing to me.
2. **Tell me the score from the start.** I only found out about "Public Trust" when Abang got 3 and I got 0. Show a little scoreboard with our four names so I know who is winning and how to get points. Also give everybody a tip, not just Abang.
3. **Don't make the Spotlight fight with the green arrows.** The card with most green arrows should be the one my power works with, or the phone should say clearly "if you want to use Spotlight pick THIS card, if not pick THAT card". And if nobody is dirty, tell me BEFORE I lock, not after.
4. **Fix the waiting.** Skip "Practice step 3 of 4" (same card again). Make the TV reveal say "flipping…" instead of black boxes. And the tip box needs more than 8 seconds.
5. **Kid words.** "Dirty card" is good, I got that. Do the same for everything: "loan" = "borrow money", "credit rating" = "the country's report card", "Public Trust" = "people's points". Or make the ⋯ button say "WHAT DOES THIS WORD MEAN?" so I know to press it.
