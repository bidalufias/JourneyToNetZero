## 2. What worked

- **Joining was fast.** Code, seat, name, I AM READY — under a minute, no reading needed. Beat everyone.
- **The seat cards' "WHO YOU ARE / WHAT YOU WANT" two-liners** were enough to pick. I knew "biggest company" was the one I wanted.
- **Cards with arrows.** Green down-arrow on carbon, "Dirty card. It breaks moving together." — I could compare cards in two seconds without reading the description.
- **"TAP A CARD FIRST" → "LOCK MY CARD" → "Locked. Now look up."** Very clear, felt like a game. "You can still change your card" was reassuring.
- **The deal flow paid off visibly.** I offered "…if the Government pays half", the card text changed from "Only half works unless the Government pays half" to "The Government pays half. It works in full.", and the reveal showed "BOTH KEPT THE DEAL ✓". That was the best moment — I could prove to Dad the deal worked.
- **The tip was a real thing.** "The next crisis is about power" → Round 2 was "Blackout Season". Made me feel like I had inside info and the family noticed.
- **My own result page** said plain things: "Cut carbon by 10.6 million tonnes. The biggest carbon cut this round." I understood I did best.
- **The practice round let me try the dirty card safely.** I learned what "breaks moving together" means from the TV instead of from text.

## 3. What did not work

- **Money maths is wrong or unexplained.** Card said "1 Company Money", the Government "pays half", yet my MONEY went **5 → 3**. Dad's Budget also never moved ("my budget still says 4"). Nobody in the room could work out where the 2 went. The result page said "WHAT IT COST: It worked as planned." — that is not a number. My whole secret goal (Market Leader, "6 or more Company Money") depends on this and I now have no idea if I am on track.
- **"PRACTICE · STEP 3 OF 4" had no button.** It said "1 OF 4 READY. The game moves on when everyone has read theirs." but there was nothing to tap. I thought my phone was broken. Every other screen had a big button, so this one felt like a bug.
- **The tip box had its own 0:16 countdown on top of the crisis screen.** Two clocks, two boxes, three buttons (SHARE IT / KEEP IT SECRET / GOT IT) all at once, and I had about 15 seconds. I tapped SHARE IT without knowing what "Public Trust" was. Aisyah and Mum then asked "how do I share a tip?" — they never got one, so it looked unfair to them.
- **After SHARE IT there was no confirmation.** Nothing on my phone said "shared" or "+1 Public Trust" until the end-of-round result. I had to check the TV to see it under "SHARED WITH EVERYONE".
- **Public Trust is never shown on my phone.** The result said "You got 1 Public Trust" and the TV showed I have 3, but my top bar only shows MONEY. Where do I look?
- **Saying one sentence takes three taps** (SAY IT → sentence type → words → who). In a 1-minute practice talk that ate most of the clock. Mum said the same.
- **"HELPED · WORKED TWICE AS WELL" on Mum's card, both in practice and Round 1, with nobody sending her anything.** Nobody in the room could explain what "helped" meant. The phrase suggests someone sent money; they did not.
- **The ⋯ help is a wall of text.** "WORDS ON YOUR SCREEN" has 15 definitions then "HOW A ROUND GOES" then my card again then "THE FULL WRITTEN GUIDE". I closed it in two seconds. Not usable mid-round with a clock running.
- **In the practice reveal my dirty card "worked".** Carbon still went from 100 to 80, economy went up. My phone showed nothing that said my card hurt anything; only the TV (which Mum read) said "With all four it would have been 9.5 million tonnes". So I learned "dirty is fine" first and had to be corrected by my parents.
- **The TV said "No money is being sent" while Dad had pressed "pay half".** Two different things (pay half vs SEND MONEY) that both sound like money, and the TV line made me think Dad had not done anything.
- **Mum's cards did not appear until after she pressed I AM DONE TALKING** (she said so twice) while mine appeared inside the SAY IT deal flow. Different phones showed different things at the same step.

## 4. Words or phrases I did not understand or skipped

- "Public Trust" — tapped SHARE IT for it without knowing what it was; still not sure what it buys me.
- "Partnership. Only half works unless the Government pays half." — understood roughly, but "half works" of what?
- "It breaks moving together." — skipped until the TV showed the grey square.
- "Clean Economy 19% / 21%" and "Every 1% of growth now adds 0.8 million tonnes of carbon." — skipped completely, TV-only.
- "IF NOTHING CHANGES, IN 2050 / 122 Mt" — did not know if that is good or bad.
- "Mt" — Aisyah asked; Dad answered, not the screen.
- "Credit rating", "lender", "strict conditions" — Dad explained, I would have skipped.
- "Cabinet Situation Room" — no idea why that heading was on a Company Boss phone.
- "THIS IS TRUE" on the tip — true compared to what? Are other things false?
- "Spotlight", "Veto", "Protest card" — only understood from Mum reading the word list out loud.
- "TWO LINES YOU CAN SAY" — never opened it. Aisyah did and called it "a press release".
- "Sawit Prima Group" — assumed it's my company name, never explained.
- "needs 5.4 a round from here" under ECONOMY — did not get what "a round" means for a percentage.

## 5. Top 5 changes I would ask for

1. **Show the money maths on my result screen.** "You paid 1. Government paid 0.5. You got +1 for the round. Now 3." Right now 5 → 3 looks like the game cheated me, and my secret goal is about money.
2. **Put Public Trust (and my secret goal progress) in the top bar** next to MONEY. Don't give me a point I can't see.
3. **One clock, one box at a time.** Don't stack the tip with its own 16-second timer on top of the crisis screen. Show the tip first, big, then the news. And give every player a tip sometimes — Aisyah and Mum felt cheated.
4. **Confirm actions instantly**: "Shared. +1 Public Trust", "Government paid half: you now pay 0.5", "Locked". And never show a screen with a status line but no button (STEP 3 OF 4).
5. **Make talking faster**: put the 3–4 most likely sentences for my role as one-tap chips ("Ba pay half?", "I'm going clean") instead of three menus, and put SEND MONEY and "pay half" on the same screen with a one-line difference.

Bonus: replace "HELPED · WORKED TWICE AS WELL" with who/what helped ("Doubled by the Government's bus card").

## 6. Was it fun?

Yes, more than I expected. The reveal is the fun bit — all four cards flipping on the TV, "BOTH KEPT THE DEAL ✓", the carbon number dropping, and getting "The biggest carbon cut this round" on my phone while Dad got 0 Public Trust for his taxes. Making a deal with Dad on the TV and then it actually being checked by the game was satisfying, and beating Aisyah on trust points was the highlight.

Boring bits: the briefing screen where the phone just says "Look up." and I wait for Dad to press NEXT; the lobby waiting for Mum every single time; the ⋯ essay; and the talk phase when I had already said my one thing and had to wait for "3 OF 4 DONE".

Annoying bits: the money disappearing with no explanation, and the tip timer.

Would I play again? Yes, if it's the same family and I can stay Company Boss — I want to see if I can hit 6 money. I would not read the guide next time either, so the phone needs to tell me the numbers as they happen.
