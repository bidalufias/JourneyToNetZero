## 2. What worked

- **The seat cards.** "Read the two lines, then tap one" with "WHO YOU ARE / WHAT YOU WANT" was clear enough that even Aisyah picked hers and put in her name before I had finished reading.
- **The arrows on the cards.** Green down-arrow for carbon, green up for quality of life; a clear label "Dirty card. It breaks moving together." on the bad ones. I knew immediately which card to pick both times, and so did Aisyah ("March one got red arrows, Publish one got three green arrows, so I pick Publish").
- **"BOTH KEPT THE DEAL ✓" on the reveal.** This made the point about promises better than any lecture. Azman and Danial were genuinely proud of their ticks, Aisyah kept her promise because of it.
- **The green "Three of you moved together" screen** with Danial's square greyed out and "With all four it would have been 9.5 million tonnes." That is a good teaching moment; Danial argued his dirty card "no problem what", and the screen answered him.
- **The private line** ("ONLY YOU CAN SEE THIS — Three people on your street lost their jobs before lunch.") gave me something to say in my role straight away.
- **The word list under ⋯** exists and is complete. I used it to answer Aisyah's Spotlight question.
- **The role's "TWO LINES YOU CAN SAY"** — "Explain it to me like I have to pay for it. Because I do." — was fun and I used it on my husband.

## 3. What did not work

1. **"HOW TO PLAY" on the join page did nothing.** I tapped it, the text turned red, no page opened. For the one person in the family who wanted to read instructions first, that is the worst possible button to be broken. I never found the "full written guide" until later, buried at the bottom of the ⋯ panel as "THE FULL WRITTEN GUIDE".

2. **The practice Talk ran out while I was reading the word list.** The TV said "Tap ⋯ to see what a word means", so I did, and the glossary is very long (about 20 entries). Nothing warned me that the 0:56 practice clock had started underneath. When I closed it there were 44 seconds left.

3. **"SAY NO" is the veto, but the button does not say so.** The button reads "SAY NO · 2 LEFT". I thought it was another sentence like "I want you to…". Only after opening it did I read "You take one player's dirty cards away for this round. … You have 2 for the whole game." I went to cancel and the clock ended before CANCEL registered — the panel vanished and I was thrown to the card screen without ever pressing I AM DONE. It also does not say whether using it in practice would have spent a real veto ("This does not count" vs "You have 2 for the whole game").

4. **I could not see my cards during the Talk.** During Round 1's "THE TALK", Azman read out "Raise Taxes, Accept the Loan, Sell power company shares", Danial read out his rail card, Aisyah read out three cards. My phone showed only "SAY IT / SAY NO / I AM DONE TALKING". My three cards appeared only after I tapped I AM DONE TALKING. So I was negotiating blind and could not tell anyone what I would pick. Either the Community seat is different or something went wrong; either way the phone never explained it.

5. **"Works twice as well if the Government or Business helps" — what is "helps"?** My card said "HELPED · WORKED TWICE AS WELL" both times, but Azman said "I didn't send you anything" and the TV said "No money is being sent." My result screen said "Another player's card made yours work better." Nobody in the room could say what triggered it. Danial called it "weird".

6. **Money confusion for the others (that I overheard).** Azman: "It says costs 1 Budget but my budget still shows 4, don't know when it takes." Then: "my budget didn't move and yours dropped 2. Something is wrong with this money, I don't understand it." Danial: "my money went 5 to 3?? The card said 1 Company Money and Ba paid half, so why minus 2?" Neither of them understood what PAYING HALF actually did.

7. **The secret goal "Nobody Left Behind — Quality of life never falls below 6.3."** It is 6.0 today. So it is already below 6.3. Does that mean I have already failed, or does it start counting later? I picked it anyway because it fitted my role, but I do not know if it can be achieved.

8. **Sharing a tip.** The TV said "The Business shared a tip. That is worth 1 Public Trust." Aisyah and Azman both asked "how did you share a tip?" I never saw any share button on my phone. Aisyah: "NOT FAIR! … Abang is winning?!" The game gave one player a scoring action the others could not find.

9. **Being the last one, every time.** "STILL READING Suraya", "Suraya, you the last one again", "Mum press done lah, you the last one again". The person who reads everything is punished by the clocks and by the family. There is no "I am still reading" signal to the others except the TV shaming me.

10. **The Public Trust rule for my seat is stated three separate times** ("You cannot hold Public Trust", "The Community holds vetoes, not Public Trust", "The Community cannot hold Public Trust. Your power is the veto.") but never once explains *why*, or what I am supposed to do about the cards that "need them".

## 4. Words and phrases I or the children did not understand

Words Aisyah (11) asked me out loud, in order: "Semenanjara", "activist", "movement", "Spotlights", "economy", "dirty cards" ("is the card got mud?"), "press release", "Net Zero", "enforced", "partnership", "Ministry", "Publish", "Mt", "Clean Economy 19%", "protest card", "Compromise", "Justice", "credit rating", "lender", "loan", "Conditions", "Debt Audit", "Public Trust", "tip", "data centres". That is twenty-five words in nine minutes.

Words I (44, science teacher, English second language) had to stop and think about:
- **"Moving together"** — I guessed from the green screen, but the phrase alone means nothing.
- **"Public Trust"** — the glossary says "Points the people give to whoever helped them most. Some cards need them. Two points go out every round." I still could not explain to Aisyah which cards need them or how you use them.
- **"Partnership: A Business card that only works fully if the Government pays half."** but then "PAYING HALF" on the TV did not seem to move any money.
- **"Clean Economy … It is the most useful number on the screen."** — it says it is the most useful and then I never saw it on my phone header (only Carbon / Economy / Quality of life). It is only on the TV.
- **"averaging 5.7 over 1"** and **"needs 5.4 a round from here"** on the TV — I understood "average" but Danial did not connect it to the "5% a year on average" target.
- **"Every 1% of growth now adds 0.8 million tonnes of carbon."** — Good science, but nobody in the room read it.
- **"Credit Downgrade"**, **"lender"**, **"strict conditions"**, **"spending cuts with a new name"** — fine for me and Azman, meaningless for Aisyah, and Danial (15) only half.
- **"Cabinet Situation Room"** on Danial's shared tip — what is that?
- **"Kampung Baru Jernih residents"** — my header. Nobody told me this is my group's name; I assumed.

## 5. Top 5 changes I would ask the makers for

1. **Fix "HOW TO PLAY" on the join page, and make it short.** One screen, five sentences: talk, pick a card in secret, lock, reveal, three targets or nobody wins. The full glossary can stay under ⋯, but the first button must open something.
2. **Show my cards during the Talk**, the same as the other three seats. I cannot negotiate about a card I have not seen, and "I will pick…" is one of the three sentences I am offered to say.
3. **Rename "SAY NO" to "VETO · 2 LEFT"** and put a one-line warning in the practice: "This is your real veto. Using it here does / does not spend one." Also, do not let the clock kill an open panel without cancelling it.
4. **Pause the clock, or show a warning, while a player has the ⋯ word list open** — or at least do not start the practice clock while someone is inside it. Better: an 11-year-old mode where tapping any hard word in the news (credit rating, lender, data centres) shows a one-line meaning right there.
5. **Explain the money and the "helped" rule on the result screen with numbers.** "The Government paid 1, you paid 1" or "Your card worked twice as well because the Business picked Build the Rail Line Together." Right now three of four players ended round 1 saying "I don't understand where my money went" or "who helped her?"

(Bonus: give every seat the same "share a tip" chance or explain why only Business had it. Aisyah's "NOT FAIR" was fair.)

## 6. Was it fun? Did it teach anything? Would I play again?

**Fun:** Yes, more than I expected. The best part was not on the screen; it was Azman promising "no loan" because I threatened to veto him, Aisyah refusing to break her TV promise even though a better card existed, and the whole family cheering "BOTH KEPT THE DEAL ✓". The clocks made everybody shout, which is fun for the children and tiring for me.

**Did it teach anything?** Some, honestly. The one thing that landed for everyone, including Danial, was the green screen: three clean cards cut 7 million tonnes, four would have cut 9.5, and his dirty card was the reason. That is a real lesson about collective action, and the game showed it rather than told it. The three-targets rule also landed: Azman cut carbon a lot with taxes and then watched the economy drop to 2.9% and "SHORT", and said "that's politics lah". I am still sceptical that the numbers mean much — carbon went from 100 to 59 in one round, which is not how any country works, and a science teacher notices — but the *structure* (secret cards, unenforced promises, one shared result) is a good classroom tool.

**Would I play again?** With my Form 4 class, yes, if HOW TO PLAY worked and the vocabulary was fixed, because I would be standing at the front explaining the words anyway. With Aisyah at home, not tonight — she said "I need to stop now, my eyes tired" after nine minutes and twenty-five vocabulary questions, and the game says it needs thirty-five.
