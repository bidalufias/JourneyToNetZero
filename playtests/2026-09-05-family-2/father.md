# Azman's Playtest Report — Journey to Net Zero (first play, Saturday evening, family of four)

Seat: Government · Minister (me, Azman, 46). Wife Suraya took Community, son Danial (15) took Business, daughter Aisyah (11) took Activist. I ran the TV from the laptop and played on my own phone. Played from lobby through the practice, Round 1 ("The Credit Downgrade") and into the start of Round 2 ("Blackout Season"). About nine minutes of game clock.

## 2. What worked

- **Joining was fast.** Code on TV, type it, seat cards appear. No account, no name until after picking a seat. Whole family was in within about a minute even with an 11-year-old.
- **The seat cards told me who to be in two lines.** "You run the government / A growing economy, and to stay in power." I knew instantly which one was mine.
- **Green/red arrows on cards.** Even Aisyah worked out "green good, red bad" on her own. I picked the bus card in practice in two seconds because every arrow pointed the right way.
- **`Dirty card. It breaks moving together.`** as a label on the card itself. I did not fully understand "moving together" but I understood "do not pick this if you want to be a team".
- **The TV telling me what to say (`SAY THIS`).** Twice the TV gave me a sentence to read to the family. As the one holding the laptop, that helped me feel like the host without knowing the rules.
- **`BOTH KEPT THE DEAL ✓` on the reveal.** Very satisfying. Danial and I made a deal, both kept it, and the TV stamped it. That was the best moment of the night.
- **The practice round being explicitly "does not count".** Danial deliberately picked the smoke card to see what happens, and nobody was punished for it. Good.
- **Phone says "Look up" when the action is on the TV.** Kept my head in the right place.

## 3. What did not work

- **`FACILITATOR SCRIPT F` did nothing I could see.** I pressed F on the laptop; the TV stayed the same. Suraya also said "I pressed HOW TO PLAY and nothing opened." Two help buttons, both dead ends for us.
- **I could not see my cards during The Talk.** Phone showed only `SAY IT / SEND MONEY / I AM DONE TALKING`. I was supposed to negotiate about cards I had not seen. I only discovered them by tapping `SAY IT` → `I will pick… if you…`, which happens to show the cards as step one. Suraya never found this route and complained "all of you can see your cards but my phone shows no cards, only the talk". She locked her card blind-ish.
- **Three taps to say one sentence, on a running clock.** Danial said it, Suraya said it, I felt it. `SAY IT` → pick type → pick words → pick who. During the 1:00 practice talk the clock was at 0:14 by the time my one sentence appeared.
- **Money is a black box.** My phone said `Budget: 4. You get 2 more each round.` My card said `+2 Budget`. Paying half said `Costs 1 Budget`. After all that, my Budget still showed **4** at the start of Round 2. Meanwhile Danial's Company Money went 5→3 for a card that "only costs 1", and he kept asking "Ba did you really pay or not?" The TV showed both `PAYING HALF` and `MONEY BEING SENT — No money is being sent.` at the same time, so we argued about whether I had paid. I still do not know.
- **`SEND MONEY` vs `I will pay half of a Business partnership`.** Two different buttons that both sound like giving money to Danial. I used one; he wanted the other. Nobody could explain the difference.
- **Public Trust appeared with no warning and I got zero.** I picked the clean card, kept my deal, cut 6.3 million tonnes, and the screen said `Government 0 · Business 3`. Aisyah shouted "NOT FAIR!" and honestly I agreed. Danial got a point for "sharing a tip" through a button that only appeared on HIS phone. Suraya: "my phone never showed me a share button, only Danial got one."
- **My secret goal died in Round 1.** `Steady Hand — The economy never grows less than 4% in any round.` The only clean card I had was `Raise Taxes` with economy ▼▼▼. Economy went to 2.9%. So either I pick dirty and get vetoed/spotlighted, or I pick clean and lose my secret goal on the first card. Felt like a trap.
- **Reveal shows cards one at a time, and the kids panicked.** Aisyah: "mine and Mum's one is black, empty! Where is my card?" Nobody told us it was a slow flip.
- **Practice showed `HELPED · WORKED TWICE AS WELL` on Suraya's card and none of us knew why.** Danial: "Who helped her? Nobody sent her anything." It happened again in Round 1. Suraya guessed it was because of other players' cards, not money. A guess.
- **Aisyah's Spotlight rules.** She turned it on, then learned it only works with a "protest card", then learned it "cannot choose who", then it caught nobody. She was disappointed and did not understand why she still had 3.
- **Aisyah is 11 and needed a translator for almost every screen.** Movement, activist, economy, Mt, net zero, enforced, press release, partnership, credit rating, lender, loan, conditions, debt audit, data centres, Public Trust, Compromise, Justice. Suraya spent the whole game defining words instead of playing.

## 4. Words or phrases I did not understand or had to guess

- `It breaks moving together` — guessed it means "stops the team bonus". Confirmed only after the reveal.
- `Public Trust` — first heard on Round 1's Public Trust screen. Had to guess it is a score. Still don't know what it buys.
- `Spotlights` — not on my phone; had to be told by the kids.
- `SAY NO` / vetoes — only on Suraya's phone; I asked out loud.
- `partnership` and `pay half` — Danial's `Build the Rail Line Together` is apparently a "partnership" but nothing told me which of his cards were partnerships.
- `HELPED · WORKED TWICE AS WELL` — who helped, and how?
- `Clean Economy 10%` — a fourth number on the TV that is not one of the three targets. Ignored it.
- `Every 1% of growth now adds 0.8 million tonnes of carbon.` — skimmed past it.
- `Mt` — I guessed million tonnes for Aisyah; it turned out to be right.
- `Landslide — Finish with 6 or more Public Trust` — I skipped this goal because I had never seen "Public Trust" yet.
- `Company Money` vs `Budget` — different names for each player's money; confused Danial when comparing.

## 5. Top 5 changes I would ask the makers for

1. **Show me my cards on the Talk screen, always.** Do not hide them behind `SAY IT → I will pick… if you…`. Put the three cards at the top and the talk buttons under them. Suraya negotiated an entire round without seeing what she could pick.
2. **Make the money visible and honest.** When I press pay-half, take the 1 Budget off my number right away and show `Budget 3 (1 promised to Business)`. When a round starts, show `+2` animate in. Right now my number never moved and my son thinks I cheated him.
3. **Merge or clearly separate `SEND MONEY` and `I will pay half…`.** One sentence under each: "Send money = give Budget now, no strings" / "Pay half = only if they pick a partnership card". And tell me which of Danial's cards ARE partnerships.
4. **Explain Public Trust before the first real round, not after.** One line on the Your Power screen: "Two Public Trust points are given each round to whoever helped people most and built the future most." And give every seat a "share a tip" chance, or nobody. Two of four players never saw the button.
5. **Fix the help buttons.** `FACILITATOR SCRIPT F` on the TV and `HOW TO PLAY` on the phone both did nothing for us. Either make them open something short, or remove them. A dad holding the laptop presses F expecting a script.

Bonus: one-tap "quick say" chips for the three most common sentences (`I will pick a clean card`, `Don't pick dirty`, `Pay half?`) so a sentence does not cost three taps and fifteen seconds.

## 6. Was it fun? Would I play again? What did the family say?

Yes, it was fun, more than I expected. The moment all four cards flipped clean with `BOTH KEPT THE DEAL ✓` and carbon dropped from 100 to 59, everybody cheered. The bank stuff (credit downgrade, loan conditions) made me feel like the game was made for me. I liked bossing the room, and the TV giving me lines to read helped me do that.

But I also got annoyed. I did the "right" thing, the economy tanked, my secret goal was dead after one card, my money didn't move, and my son ended up with 3 trust points to my zero. Danial said "your taxes killed it Ba, that one on you" and "I'm winning lah". Suraya said "Azman, you got 0 for taxes, the voters are angry like the card said" and then "help me with my card, you promised". Aisyah said "NOT FAIR!" about the trust points and then "I need to stop now, my eyes tired" at the start of Round 2, which is about the point an 11-year-old would give up: too many words she did not know.

Would I play again? Yes, but next time I would want to know how the money works before I make a deal, and I would want Aisyah to have a phone screen written for an 11-year-old. Thirty-five minutes is right for a Saturday; the reading load is not.
