1 · The country

You are running the Republic of Semenanjara. It is a made-up country on a hot peninsula.

34 million people live here. The capital is Kota Damai. The money is the Ringga. It is 2025, and you have until 2050.

2 · The three targets

Three numbers must reach their target by 2050. Miss one and nobody wins.

CarbonToday 100 · Target 0

The country puts out 300 million tonnes a year. Its forests take in 200. The 100 left over must reach 0. That is net zero.

EconomyToday 4.5% · Target 5% a year on average

The average over all six rounds must be 5% or more.

Quality of lifeToday 6.0 · Target 7 out of 10

Air, water, jobs, prices and safety, out of 10. A country can reach net zero and still be a bad place to live.

3 · Who you are

Four seats. You get one. Play the part.

Government

The Minister

Ministry of Energy and Climate

Who you are

The Minister. You run the government.

What you want

A growing economy, and to stay in power.

What you have

Budget: 4. You get 2 more each round. Cards cost 1 to 3 Budget.

How to play

Spend on big changes. Give Budget to the Business when a deal is worth it. Always ask what the voters get.

Business

The Company Boss

Sawit Prima Group

Who you are

The Company Boss. You own the biggest company in the country.

What you want

Profit, and a company that still exists in 2050.

What you have

Company Money: 5. You get 1 more each round. Going clean costs 3.

How to play

Go clean when someone helps you pay. Ask the Government to pay half. Protect your workers’ jobs.

Community

The Community Leader

Kampung Baru Jernih residents

Who you are

The Community Leader. You speak for 34 million ordinary people.

What you want

Clean air, and prices people can afford.

What you have

2 vetoes. A veto takes one player’s dirty cards away for one round.

How to play

Ask what each plan costs ordinary people. Remind players what they promised. Use a veto when someone is about to hurt everyone.

Activist

The Youth Activist

Bangkit Iklim

Who you are

The Youth Activist. You lead the biggest movement in the country.

What you want

Real climate action, fast.

What you have

3 Spotlights. A Spotlight punishes a player who picks a dirty card.

How to play

Push everyone to pick clean cards. Offer deals: “I will support you if you go clean”. Use a Spotlight when you think someone will pick a dirty card.

4 · How a round works

Every round has the same four steps. A practice round comes first and does not count.

0:25

The Crisis

The big screen shows the news. Your phone shows it too, plus one line only you can see. Tap GOT IT when you have read it.

1:30

The Talk

Your cards stay on your phone. Tap SAY IT to put a promise or a deal on the big screen. Nobody has to keep a promise. Tap I AM DONE when you have said your part.

0:40

The Choice

Three cards on your phone. The four arrows show what each card does to the country. Green is good. Tap one card, then tap LOCK MY CARD. If the clock runs out, the card you tapped is used.

0:45

The Reveal

All four cards turn over on the big screen, and on your phone. The meters move. If someone broke a promise, the screen says so. Then your phone says what your card did, and where your money went.

After six rounds, the country gets its result. Reach all three targets and everyone wins. Miss one and nobody wins. A player who reached their secret goal gets HOLLOW VICTORY instead.

5 · Words on your screen

Spotlight
Pick a protest card. Then any dirty card the Government or Business picks only half works.
Protest card
A loud card. The Spotlight only works with one of these.
Veto
You take one player’s dirty cards away for this round.
Public Trust
Points the people give to whoever helped them most. Two go out every round. Promises do not count.
Clean Economy
How much of the economy is clean. Higher means growth adds less carbon.
Moving together
When 3 or 4 of you pick good cards, the country gets a bonus.
Dirty card
A card that pollutes or makes others pay. It breaks moving together.
Partnership
A Business card that only works fully if the Government pays half.
Net zero
Carbon at 0. The country takes out as much as it puts in.
Secret goal
A goal only you know. It only counts if the country reaches all three targets.
Tip
A true warning about the next crisis. Only you can see it. One player gets one each round, in turn.
