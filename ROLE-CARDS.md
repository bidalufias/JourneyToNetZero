# Journey to Net Zero: the role card

The complaint: it takes too long to read and understand who your character
is, what they want, what they can do, and how to play them.

This document says why, and replaces the five screens a player reads today
with one card of about sixty words, shown in the same shape at every moment
the player needs it.

`LANGUAGE-REVIEW.md` sets the writing standard the cards follow.
`FLOW-REVIEW.md` says where in the session they appear.

---

## 1. Why it takes too long

A player learns their role from five screens, at five different moments,
under five different sets of headings.

| When | Screen | What it says | Words |
|---|---|---|---|
| Joining | Seat button | "Government · Minister" | 2 |
| Taking the seat | Role reveal | You are, You want, Your move, You hold, three things you might say | 78 to 86 |
| Same screen, one tap | "More about me" | Who you are, What you believe, What you fear, How your resource works, You would never say | 124 to 155 |
| Four minutes later | Your power | Your move again, then How it works | 67 to 87 |
| One minute after that | Secret goal | Three goals | 28 to 38 |
| Any time | ⋯ How to play | Everything again, in different words | 738 shared |

Four problems, in order of damage.

1. **The most important line is missing.** Nothing on the phone tells a
   player how to play their seat. "How to play it" exists only in the
   4,000-word written guide. So a player knows who they are and does not
   know what to do.
2. **The same fact is told three times in three shapes.** The Community's
   veto is explained on the reveal ("Twice a game you can say no and mean
   it"), again on the power step in 84 words, and again in the ⋯ sheet. Each
   version uses different words, so it reads as three facts.
3. **Every screen has different headings.** "You are", "Your move", "You
   hold", "How it works", "What you spend". A reader cannot build a picture
   when the boxes keep changing.
4. **The goals are the hardest words in the game**, and they arrive last.
   "Never take a Collaborate card, and still take the country 25 Mt past net
   zero" is a sentence no one at the table can act on.

Reading load per player before Round 1: 230 to 271 words about their own
role, plus 738 shared, spread over four minutes. At a careful second-language
pace that is five to eight minutes of reading, inside a four-minute
onboarding.

## 2. The role card

One card. Four fixed lines. The same four headings everywhere, in the same
order, on every surface. A fifth line is added when the secret goal is
chosen.

```
WHO YOU ARE      one sentence
WHAT YOU WANT    one sentence
WHAT YOU HAVE    your money or power, with the number
HOW TO PLAY      three short instructions
YOUR SECRET GOAL added at the goal step, one line
```

Rules: 70 words or fewer. Every sentence has a subject and a verb. No game
word without its meaning beside it. Written to the plain standard.

### Government

> **WHO YOU ARE** · The Minister. You run the government.
> **WHAT YOU WANT** · A growing economy, and to stay in power.
> **WHAT YOU HAVE** · Budget: 4. You get 2 more each round. Cards cost 1 to 3 Budget.
> **HOW TO PLAY** · Spend on big changes. Give Budget to the Business when a deal is worth it. Always ask what the voters get.

### Business

> **WHO YOU ARE** · The Company Boss. You own the biggest company in the country.
> **WHAT YOU WANT** · Profit, and a company that still exists in 2050.
> **WHAT YOU HAVE** · Company Money: 5. You get 1 more each round. Going clean costs 3.
> **HOW TO PLAY** · Go clean when someone helps you pay. Ask the Government to pay half. Protect your workers' jobs.

### Community

> **WHO YOU ARE** · The Community Leader. You speak for 34 million ordinary people.
> **WHAT YOU WANT** · Clean air, and prices people can afford.
> **WHAT YOU HAVE** · 2 vetoes. A veto takes one player's dirty cards away for one round.
> **HOW TO PLAY** · Ask what each plan costs ordinary people. Remind players what they promised. Use a veto when someone is about to hurt everyone.

### Activist

> **WHO YOU ARE** · The Youth Activist. You lead the biggest movement in the country.
> **WHAT YOU WANT** · Real climate action, fast.
> **WHAT YOU HAVE** · 3 Spotlights. A Spotlight punishes a player who picks a dirty card.
> **HOW TO PLAY** · Push everyone to pick clean cards. Offer deals: "I will support you if you go clean." Use a Spotlight when you think someone will pick a dirty card.

Word counts: 52, 49, 58, 62.

### Two lines you can say

Kept, because shy players use them, but cut from three to two and written
plain. Shown under the card behind one tap, never on the card itself.

| Role | Line 1 | Line 2 |
|---|---|---|
| Government | I can pay for that. What do I tell the voters? | If I do that, I lose the election. Then nothing happens. |
| Business | I will do it if the Government pays half. | Who pays to retrain my workers? |
| Community | Explain it to me like I have to pay for it. Because I do. | You promised this last time. What happened? |
| Activist | That is not a plan. That is a press release. | I will support you in public if you promise in public. |

### The secret goals

Three per role. Twelve words or fewer, with today's number where the goal is
a number.

| Role | Goal | Line on the card |
|---|---|---|
| Government | Landslide | Finish with 6 or more Public Trust. |
| Government | Steady Hand | The economy never grows less than 4% in any round. |
| Government | The Legacy | Clean Economy reaches 55%. It is 10% today. |
| Business | Market Leader | Finish with 6 or more Company Money. |
| Business | Green Champion | Your own cards cut 33 million tonnes of carbon or more. |
| Business | Trusted Company | Finish with 2 or more Public Trust and 4 or more Company Money. |
| Community | A Happy Nation | Quality of life reaches 8. It is 6 today. |
| Community | Nobody Left Behind | Quality of life never falls below 6.3. |
| Community | Our Own Hands | 4 of your community cards get real help from the Government or Business. |
| Activist | No Compromise | Never pick a "join them" card, and carbon still reaches 25 million tonnes below zero. |
| Activist | The Long Game | Clean Economy reaches 52%. It is 10% today. |
| Activist | Justice First | Quality of life reaches 7, and one of your Spotlights catches someone. |

Once chosen, the goal is the fifth line of the card, everywhere the card is
shown.

## 3. Where the card appears

The same card, five times. A player reads sixty words several times instead
of two hundred and fifty words once.

| Moment | What is shown | Reading time |
|---|---|---|
| Seat picking | Each seat button carries WHO YOU ARE and WHAT YOU WANT. Two lines per seat, so the seat is chosen with eyes open. | 10 seconds for all four |
| Taking the seat | The full card, large, in the role colour, then I AM READY. "Two lines you can say" behind one tap. | 25 seconds |
| Your power step | WHAT YOU HAVE and HOW TO PLAY, large, on the phone. On the projector, all four cards side by side, so every player learns what the other three can do to them. | 20 seconds |
| Secret goal step | The three goals as three buttons. After choosing, the card with its fifth line. | 20 seconds |
| Any time | The ⋯ menu opens on the card, goal included. Then the glossary. Nothing else. | as needed |

Everything the card does not carry goes to the facilitator page: the
backstory, what the character believes and fears, the strategy notes. A
facilitator can read those aloud when a table wants colour. A player never
has to.

## 4. What changes in the code

- `ROLE_CHARACTER` in `src/game/session.ts` becomes four fields plus two
  say-lines: `who`, `wants`, `has`, `howToPlay`, `says`. The backstory
  fields move to the facilitator script.
- One `RoleCard` component renders the card on the phone and the projector.
  The reveal, the power step, the goal confirmation and the ⋯ menu all use
  it. Today four screens each lay the role out by hand.
- The seat buttons on the join page show two lines from the card.
- The projector's power step shows four `RoleCard`s instead of one heading.
- The goal lines in the content pack are rewritten as in section 2.
- The how-to-play sheet on the phone opens with the card and drops its own
  role paragraph.

This is small, and it belongs in Stage A of the flow overhaul, since Stage A
already reworks the power and goal steps.
